Why We Do This

Real estate is ultimately a relationship business. The best partnerships, referrals, and opportunities usually begin with a simple conversation — not a seminar.

Deals on Draft exists to give Pittsburgh's real estate community a relaxed place to meet one another. Everyone is welcome.

Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Is this educational? +
No. There are no presentations, no speakers, and no agenda. It's a happy hour, not a class.
Are there speakers or a program? +
Nope. Just people, conversations, and a good time. Show up whenever, leave whenever.
Is it really free? +
Yes. Food and beverages are provided by our community sponsors. Come as our guest.
Can beginners attend? +
Absolutely. Some attendees own hundreds of properties. Others don't own their first one yet. Everyone belongs.
Can I bring guests? +
Yes — bring a colleague, a partner, or a friend who's curious about Pittsburgh real estate.
Do I need business cards? +
Optional. The emphasis is on real conversations, not swapping stacks of cards.
